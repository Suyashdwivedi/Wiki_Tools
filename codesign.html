CodeSign: Text to ASL Fingerspelling

Type text and see the American Sign Language (ASL) hand shapes letter by letter.

Spells your text letter by letter in the American Sign Language alphabet (fingerspelling).

These are simplified drawings. Letters like M, N, S, T and E look alike, so read the written hint and check an ASL chart. J and Z are moving letters: follow the red dashed path. Photos show one signer; J and Z are moving letters. Many words also have their own ASL sign that differs from spelling; this tool only fingerspells.
