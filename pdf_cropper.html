PDF Cropper: Local PDF Cropper & Merger

Crop PDF pages to your chosen aspect ratio and combine them into one PDF. Your PDF files stay on your device.

1. Select a PDF

Drag and drop PDF files here
or click to choose files above
Select one or several PDFs (for example, 10 files). Each input PDF becomes its own cropped PDF (for example, 1.pdf → 1_cropped.pdf). To avoid multiple-download browser blocks, all output PDFs are delivered together in one ZIP file.
Choose a PDF from your computer to begin.

2. Set the crop ratio

On the preview, drag to draw the crop rectangle. The selected ratio is enforced while drawing. The same proportional crop is applied to every page.
